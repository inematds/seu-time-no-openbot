How it works

A small office, without jargon

Picture an office with four people. OpenBot is the building; the AI you subscribe to is each person’s brain; the kit is their training.

🧭 The manager (orchestrator)

You talk to it. It splits up your request, gives it to the right person, and comes back with an answer. To bring in someone new, it first shows you who they are and what they will do, and creates them only after you say yes.

📒 The account notebook (finance assistant)

A simple table in its folder: date, amount, what it was, category, and payment method. Every week, it adds things up and tells the story.

🗓️ The planner (organizer)

A task list with deadlines only when you set them. It picks today’s 3 tasks and the first step for each one.

🔍 The checker (reviewer)

Like a cashier checking the change: the person who did the work does not check their own work. It recalculates and shows evidence for each mistake.

🏢 Why “24 hours a day”?

OpenBot can run on a rented server (a VPS, a computer that stays on in a data center). Then the team keeps working while your computer is off, and you talk to it from your phone or browser. On your own computer, the team works while it is on.

⚠️ What the kit does not promise

  • It does not make AI smarter: it is the AI you subscribe to.
  • It does not pay, transfer money, or access a bank.
  • It has not been tested inside OpenBot with the agents talking to each other; the files follow the official format and pass the kit’s checker.

Safety

What to change before letting the team work alone

OpenBot itself warns that, by default, agents can edit files, run commands, and use the internet without asking at every step. That is power, and also risk. Five precautions address most of it.

✋ Ask first

Each agent has a permission level. Change “Full access” to “Ask first” for all four. That way, a routine can wait for your approval before acting. An agent you create from another inherits its limits.

📱 Use a separate WhatsApp SIM

OpenBot’s WhatsApp integration is unofficial, and WhatsApp may block the number. Use a SIM just for the team, never your important number.

👤 A separate AI account

The team uses your subscription, and the provider may limit an account it thinks has gone beyond normal use. If your account is for work or is important, use another one for the team.

🔑 Keep passwords in a vault

Passwords, card details, and codes do not belong in the conversation. The kit’s skills already refuse to save them; use a password manager.

💾 Back up every day

The kit includes a full backup: it stops the program, copies everything together, restarts, and keeps the 7 newest copies, deleting the older ones. To make this daily, schedule it on the server once (the README shows how). Keep a copy off the server.

📜 The license

OpenBot is free for personal use. If the team works for your company or clients, that is commercial use and requires its paid license.

The complete list, with a documentation page for each item, is in the security checklist (in Portuguese).

Common questions

Before you start

Do I need to know how to code?

Not to use the team: you talk to it. For a server installation, it helps to have someone technical or ask your AI to guide you through the README steps.

How much does it cost?

The kit is free. OpenBot is free for personal use. Use the AI subscription you already have, or the models marked as free in OpenCode (one of the AI programs OpenBot can use, with no login). Your own computer is enough for testing. A rented server to keep the team running 24 hours a day costs extra each month, paid to the provider you choose.

Can I use it at my company?

Commercial use of OpenBot requires its paid license (Starter and Team plans, in OpenBot’s own settings). The kit does not change that.

Am I allowed to use my subscription?

It depends on the provider. OpenBot itself says that with Claude through a subscription, the risk ranges from “unclear” to “high” when other people use your agents. Read your provider’s rules, and if the account is important, use another one.

Could WhatsApp block me?

It could. OpenBot uses an unofficial WhatsApp integration. That is why you should use a separate SIM, just for the team.

What if an agent makes a mess?

With “ask first,” it requests permission. The kit’s skills do not send anything out without your yes and do not delete anything you did not ask them to. And if you installed it on the server and scheduled daily backups, you can roll back.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

If OpenBot runs on your computer, yes. To run it 24 hours a day, use a server; the steps are in the README.

Can I change the agents?

Yes. Each agent’s instructions are in Portuguese, in plain text. Change anything you like, or ask the orchestrator to create a new agent.
